One night in Madrid

Saturday, Sep 26. They land at 8:05 AM after the overnight flight, so this plan starts late afternoon, once they've had a chance to freshen up. Madrid eats dinner very late, so a snack comes first.

  1. 🚣

    4:30 PMRetiro Park

    Madrid's giant park has a big pond where you can rent a rowboat, and a glass palace (the Palacio de Cristal) that shines in the afternoon sun.

    Kid challenge: count how many rowboats are on the water.
  2. 🐻

    6:00 PMPuerta del Sol and Plaza Mayor

    Find the bear reaching up to a strawberry tree, Madrid's symbol, and the plaque marking Kilometer Zero, the spot all of Spain's roads are measured from. Plaza Mayor is a huge square with painted buildings all around.

  3. 🌇

    7:30 PMSunset at the Temple of Debod

    Yes, a real ancient Egyptian temple, more than 2,000 years old, moved to Madrid. Sunset is around 8:05 PM, and the palace and park nearby glow gold.

  4. 🍽️

    9:30 PMLate Spanish dinner

    Spanish families often eat at 9 or 10 PM. Two good ideas:

    Option A, the classic: Sobrino de Botín, listed by Guinness as the world's oldest restaurant (since 1725), famous for roast suckling pig. Reserve ahead.
    Option B, the snack crawl: tapas at the Mercado de San Miguel, sampling little plates from stall to stall.
  5. 🍫

    11:00 PMChurros con chocolate

    Chocolatería San Ginés has been serving hot chocolate thick enough to dunk churros into since 1894. It's a Madrid night-out tradition.

  6. ⚽

    If there's energyPeek at the Bernabéu

    Real Madrid's stadium is lit up at night and only a short metro ride from downtown. It's a great photo stop for any soccer fan.

  7. 😴

    MidnightLights out

    Bed before midnight, because the high-speed train to the coast leaves in the morning.

Why Marbella?

It's home base for the Killarney Cup, a yearly golf match between Team Ireland and Team USA that has been played since 2018. Four rounds, two teams, one Cup.

Marbella sits on the Costa del Sol ("Sun Coast") in southern Spain, right along the Mediterranean Sea. The whitewashed Old Town has narrow streets, flower pots and lots of little plazas.
